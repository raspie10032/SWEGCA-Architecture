# Known limitations

The standalone repository intentionally preserves the paper's limits:

1. proposal evidence addresses may be empty;
2. accepted decision history is not bound to proposal addresses;
3. address membership would still not prove tensor-semantic binding;
4. different source strings are only a conflict proxy, not proof of independent evidence;
5. low-level arbitration remains authority-sensitive;
6. registered tests are development regression evidence, not held-out natural-world confirmation;
7. recovery is a local SQLite/versioned-memory protocol, not distributed atomicity; and
8. status-unfiltered experience usefulness has not been confirmed on a fresh held-out comparison.

Do not describe zero test failures as general safety, factual correctness, or complete evidence gating.
